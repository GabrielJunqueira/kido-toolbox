Event Polygon Optimizer

Mobile network data lands on z=18 projection nodes, and the nodes that record an event are not always the ones closest to it. This tool measures the real per-node attendance on a past event day and proposes a polygon backed by evidence, not by eye.

Defaults come from measurements on production data. Change them only when you know why.

Hours 0-2 are inflated by people already in the area.

Hour 23 is structurally depressed by the day cutoff.

Hour 23 is under-counted by construction: a stay starting at 23:00 has little time to reach the minimum duration before the day ends. Include it only for events that run past midnight.
Score weights

Without a baseline day the weights shift to 0.30 / 0.35 / 0.20 / 0.15, because excess gets less reliable and the shape of the curve matters more.
